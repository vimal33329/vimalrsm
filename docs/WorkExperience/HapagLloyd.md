# Hapag Lloyd

## Customer Support

🏢 **Company:** Hapag Lloyd — Cargo Import/Export and Shipping company  
📍 **Location:** Chennai, India  
📅 **Duration:** 11/2015 – 05/2018

## Responsibilities

- Designed and implemented UI enhancements to streamline cargo import and export processes, ensuring efficient and user-friendly workflows.
- Collaborated with clients to gather requirements, troubleshoot UI bugs, and deliver intuitive solutions to enhance the user experience.
- Developed and optimized the shipment tracking system, ensuring compliance with international shipping regulations and seamless integration across platforms.
- Coordinated with internal teams to debug and resolve front-end and back-end inconsistencies, ensuring accurate shipment documentation and smooth customs clearance.
- Effectively communicated with stakeholders, including logistics teams and external agents, to resolve UI and functionality-related issues, improving operational efficiency.
